Install Npcap
Download it from the official site: https://npcap.com/#download
Run the installer as Administrator.
Important: during installation, check the box "Install Npcap in WinPcap API-compatible Mode" — scapy relies on this for compatibility.
Leave the other options at default (WinPcap-compatible + loopback support are usually fine as-is).
Finish the install, then reboot your machine (Npcap's driver sometimes doesn't fully register until a restart).
